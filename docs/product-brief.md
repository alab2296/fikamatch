# Product brief

**FikaMatch** is a dating app for Sweden built around intention and safety. The name comes from
_fika_, the coffee break: the product nudges people toward a low-pressure first meeting, a coffee,
in public, in daylight.

## Principles

- **Intentional**: people say what they want (relationship type, deal-breakers, pace) up front.
- **Safety first**: 18+ only, block and report, approximate location only, scam and pressure warnings.
- **Transparent AI**: the AI explains itself, is always labelled, never pretends to be a person and
  never acts without the user seeing it. Users can turn it off.
- **Privacy**: GDPR in full, EU-only data, export and real deletion, no selling data.

## Market

Sweden. Swedish first, English second. Launch cities in the seed data: Stockholm, Gothenburg,
Malmo, Uppsala. Phone numbers in +46 format. BankID verification is designed in as an interface
with a fake implementation for now.

## AI features (in build order)

1. Explained matches: a short, honest "why you two".
2. Intent interview: a short conversation at signup instead of a long form.
3. Safety guard: flags scams, pressure, harassment and money requests; warns the receiver; one-tap report.
4. First-date planner: public venues (cafes first) and times that fit both people.
5. Reply coach (optional, later): suggestions, always marked as suggestions.

## Scope of this build

Backend, tests, admin web, then a Flutter app. Portfolio quality, not a production launch.
